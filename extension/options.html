ClickSafe settings

You're protected. Nothing else to set up.

From now on, every new website opens behind the ClickSafe shield. It stays hidden and muted until it has been checked against TikTok, Twitch, YouTube and Instagram guidelines.

  1. Pin it: click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to ClickSafe.
  2. Test it: open any site you haven't allowed and watch the shield come up.
  3. Sites you use all the time are on your allowed list. Add more with one click below.

Allowed sites

Allowed sites open instantly and are never hidden or scanned. A domain covers its subdomains. Paste a domain or a full link.

    User-content areas like sites.google.com, docs.google.com and gist.github.com are always checked, even when their parent domain is allowed.

    Always blocked

    These never open, whatever the AI says.

      AI vision scan

      By default ClickSafe uses OVHcloud AI Endpoints' free anonymous tier: no account, no key, limited per IP address, so every user has their own quota (about 8 scans a minute across 4 vision models). The page's text and up to 3 of its largest images are sent for the check. Allowed sites are never sent.

      Use the AI scanOff = local checks only (blocklists, look-alike domains, page keywords).
      Send page imagesLets the model actually look at the page. Off = text only.
      Use my own AI endpointOff = the free anonymous scanner above (recommended). Turn on to use a provider you have a key for.

      Until a valid https URL and at least one model are saved, ClickSafe keeps using the free anonymous scanner.
