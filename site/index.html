How it works

Hidden first. Checked second. Shown last.

Most tools warn you after a page is already on screen. ClickSafe never lets an unchecked page paint, and you don't have to do anything: it kicks in when you click a link or a site redirects you.

Sealed preview first

Before an unchecked link even loads, it's opened in a sealed remote browser and screenshotted. Your PC never touches it.

  • Follows every redirect hop to where the link really ends up
  • The site never sees your IP and can't run code on your PC
  • Screenshot and text are checked before the page loads for you
  • Skipped for login and token links, so your accounts stay private

Then a real check, hidden

The page loads behind the shield, blurred and muted, and ClickSafe checks what it actually does, not just what it shows first.

  • Presses Play on videos and samples frames across the whole video
  • Fast-forwards the page's timers 7 minutes (you can change this), so "normal for a minute, then the surprise" tricks show up during the check
  • Reads every script, including code generated at runtime and hidden code
  • Finds every image: hidden ones, CSS backgrounds, images only named in code, and images inside embedded frames
  • Blocklists, live threat feeds and look-alike domains like dlscord

Reveal, or keep it blocked

Clean pages appear within a few seconds. Pages with video take a little longer. Anything against the guidelines stays off your screen.

  • Lockdown mode: no "show anyway" for guideline breaks
  • Images and videos that load later stay blurred until checked
  • Unsafe pages get a neutral "Blocked" screen
  • Your allowed sites skip the check
The AI

Several models. Each with one job.

One model can be fooled or unsure, so ClickSafe uses several, each looking at a different part of the page. All of them are free, with no account and no API key.

Install

Three steps, about 30 seconds.

Chrome only allows true one-click installs from the Chrome Web Store, so for now ClickSafe installs as an unpacked extension. The download button copies the address you need for step 2.

Open ClickSafe.zip from your downloads, then click Extract all. You'll get a ClickSafe folder.

Paste this into a new tab, then switch on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

3

Click Load unpacked and pick the ClickSafe folder, or just drag the folder onto the extensions page. The welcome page opens and you're protected.

Tip: pin ClickSafe (puzzle icon → pin) to see what it caught and allow sites in one click. Keep the unzipped folder where it is. Chrome loads the extension from it.

Privacy
0 servers

There is no ClickSafe server.

No account, analytics or tracking. Your lists and stats stay in your browser. Sites on your allowed list are never scanned.

When a new site is scanned, its address, visible text and a few of its images go to the AI endpoint for the check, and a short clip from the start of a video goes to speech-to-text. The sealed preview sends the link to Jina Reader, which opens it on its own servers (never for login or token links). The domain-age check asks a public registry (RDAP) about the site's domain name.

Each of these can be switched off in settings. The on-device image model sends nothing.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does it guarantee I won't get banned?

No tool can promise that. ClickSafe makes it much less likely that a bad page ever reaches your screen: it fails closed (if it can't decide, the page stays hidden) and checks against all four platforms' guidelines. Keep using your platform's own safety settings too.

How do I allow a site?

Click the ClickSafe icon and press "Allow this site", or open settings and type or paste any domain or link into Allowed sites. There are one-click suggestions for popular sites. Allowed sites open instantly and are never scanned.

What is lockdown mode?

It's on by default. When a page breaks platform guidelines, nobody can click "Show anyway" or "Open anyway" past it, not even by accident mid-stream. If you really need a site, add it to your allowed list in settings.

What about stuff that loads after the page opens?

New images and videos that appear later (infinite scroll, carousels, autoplay) stay blurred and muted until the AI clears them. If one breaks the guidelines, the whole page is blocked.

Do I need to paste links anywhere?

No. ClickSafe checks automatically when you click a link or a site redirects you. If you want to check a link without opening it, right-click it and pick "Check this link with ClickSafe".

What is the sealed preview?

Before an unchecked link loads in your browser, it's opened in a sealed remote browser (Jina Reader) that follows every redirect and takes a screenshot. The site never reaches your PC or sees your IP during that step. Links that look like logins or carry one-time tokens skip it, so nothing private is opened by someone else. The page still gets the full hidden check when it loads for you.

Will it slow down my browsing?

Allowed sites open instantly. New sites are checked behind the shield the first time, usually within a few seconds. Pages with video take longer because ClickSafe plays and samples them. Repeat visits within 30 minutes are instant.

What if something gets past it?

Press Alt+Shift+X. The panic button covers and mutes the current page instantly, so you can deal with it off stream.

What do my viewers see while a page is checked?

A dark "Checking this page" screen with the site name. If the page is blocked, they see a neutral "Blocked" screen. The reason and the address are tucked behind a collapsed "Details" section so nothing explicit ends up on stream.

Does it work without the AI?

Yes. Turn the AI scan off and ClickSafe still uses its blocklists, live threat feeds, look-alike domain detection, adult rating tags, on-page keyword checks, its script checks, the on-device image model, and the clipboard, download, IP-lookup and prompt guards.

Which browsers?

Any Chromium browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi. In Edge the address for step 2 is edge://extensions, in Brave brave://extensions. The copy button picks the right one for you.
